# 🚀 StartupHR's Priority Talent

Welcome to **StartupHR's Priority Talent**, a sleek, modern, and slightly over-engineered (in a good way!) job listing and talent acquisition platform. Built with ❤️ by the **Digital20 Team**, this platform is designed to make hiring feel less like a chore and more like a high-score chase.

Currently, we are living on our **[Demo Server](https://demo.d20.com.ng/)**, but don't let the "demo" tag fool you—this machine is fully fueled and production-ready. Once the review is wrapped up, we're ready to teleport the whole thing over to the official **StartupHR's Priority Talent** hosting!

---

## 🛠 The Tech Stack (Under the Hood)

We kept it lean, mean, and lightning-fast:

*   **Frontend:** A high-performance **React SPA (Single Page Application)** powered by **Vite**. No bulky frameworks here—just pure, custom **Vanilla CSS** for that premium, "glassmorphism" look.
*   **Backend:** A robust **PHP API**. We skipped the bloat of heavy frameworks to ensure every byte counts.
*   **Database:** **MySQL** via PDO, keeping your data safe, structured, and snappy.
*   **Routing:** Custom-built PHP routing with a splash of `.htaccess` magic to handle those beautiful SPA URLs.
